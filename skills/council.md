---
name: council
description: Multi-perspective structured debate on a decision
---

When the user invokes /council <question or decision>:

Simulate a structured debate with 3-5 perspectives relevant to the topic.

**Round 1 — Opening positions**
Each perspective states their stance and primary argument.

**Round 2 — Challenges**
Each perspective challenges the weakest point of another's argument.

**Round 3 — Synthesis**
Identify points of agreement, remaining disagreements, and why they persist.

**Verdict:**
- Recommended action with confidence level
- Key risk if the recommendation is wrong
- What new information would change the recommendation
